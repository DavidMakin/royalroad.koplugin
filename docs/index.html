KOReader Plugin

Royal Road Downloader

Search and download web fiction from Royal Road directly to your e-reader, as neatly formatted EPUB files — no computer required.

Download Latest Release Works on Kobo, Kindle, Android, Linux, and macOS

Features

On-Device Search

Search Royal Road by title from within KOReader, without leaving your device or opening a browser.

Complete Story Download

Downloads an entire fiction — all chapters, cover image, and metadata — packaged as a single EPUB.

Auto-Update Chapters

Check for new chapters and append them to existing EPUBs automatically, keeping your library current.

Cover Images and Metadata

Fetches the Royal Road cover art, author, and title so your EPUB looks polished in any reader.

Reading Position Preserved

When updating a story, your place in the book is retained — you pick up right where you left off.

Partial Download Resume

If a download is interrupted, the plugin can resume from where it stopped rather than starting over.

How to Use

1

Search

Open the plugin from the KOReader menu and type a story title. Results from Royal Road appear directly on your device.

2

Download

Select a story to begin downloading. All chapters are fetched, formatted, and saved as an EPUB with cover art and metadata.

3

Manage

Your downloaded stories appear in the plugin's library. Open them in KOReader just like any other ebook.

4

Update

When new chapters are published, tap Update to fetch and append them. Your reading position is preserved throughout.

Installation

The easiest way to install is via the KOReader App Store plugin — open it on your device, search for Royal Road Downloader, and tap Install.

To install manually: download the latest release, unzip it, and copy the royalroad.koplugin folder into your KOReader plugins directory for your device, then restart KOReader.

Device Plugin directory
Kobo /mnt/onboard/.adds/koreader/plugins/
Kindle /mnt/us/koreader/plugins/
Android /sdcard/koreader/plugins/
Linux / macOS ~/.config/koreader/plugins/

After installation the plugin is accessible under Menu → Tools → Royal Road Downloader.

Limitations

  • No official Royal Road API is used — the plugin scrapes HTML, so changes to the site's structure may break it temporarily until an update is released.
  • Paywalled, member-only, or restricted stories are not supported and cannot be downloaded.
  • Very long stories with hundreds of chapters may take several minutes to download over a slow connection.
  • The plugin does not track new Royal Road releases automatically; you must manually trigger an update check.
